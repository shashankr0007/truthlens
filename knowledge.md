## 1. About the college
- Name: RV Institute of Technology and Management (RVITM), Bengaluru, Karnataka.
- Affiliation: Visvesvaraya Technological University (VTU), Belagavi.
- Programs offered: B.E. in Information Science and Engineering (ISE), Computer Science and Engineering (CSE), Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE), Mechanical Engineering (ME), Civil Engineering (CE).
- Each B.E. program is 4 years (8 semesters).
- College working days: Monday to Saturday. Sunday and public holidays are closed.
- College hours: 8:30 AM to 4:30 PM.

## 2. Admissions
- Admission to the first year is through KEA counselling (based on KCET or COMEDK rank) and the management quota.
- Lateral entry (direct second year) is for diploma holders through the KEA lateral entry process.
- Documents needed at admission: 10th and 12th marks cards, KCET or COMEDK rank card, transfer certificate, caste and income certificate (if applicable), 6 passport-size photos, Aadhaar copy.
- Admission office hours: 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM, Monday to Friday.

## 3. Fees (SAMPLE VALUES)
- Tuition fee per year (government quota): Rs. 60,000.
- Tuition fee per year (management quota): Rs. 1,50,000.
- Exam fee is charged separately by VTU each semester.
- Fees are paid at the accounts section or through the online payment link shared by the office.
- Last date for fee payment is announced through circulars each semester. Late payment may attract a fine.
- Fee receipts are issued by the accounts section within 2 working days.
- Scholarship queries (SC/ST, minority, state scholarships) are handled by the scholarship cell in the administration block.

## 4. Attendance and exam rules
- Minimum attendance required to appear for semester end exams: 75% in each subject.
- Students with attendance between 65% and 75% may apply for condonation with a valid reason and fee, as per VTU rules.
- Students with attendance below 65% are not eligible to write the semester end exam.
- Each theory subject has Continuous Internal Evaluation (CIE) of 50 marks and Semester End Examination (SEE) of 50 marks.
- Minimum passing requirement is 40% in SEE and 40% overall in CIE plus SEE for a subject.
- Internal assessment tests are conducted 2 times per semester, and the average is considered.
- Hall ticket is issued only to students who meet attendance and fee clearance conditions.
- Carrying mobile phones into the exam hall is not allowed.
- For revaluation or photocopy of answer scripts, students must apply on the VTU portal within the dates announced by VTU.

## 5. Timetable and academic calendar
- Class timings: 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM with a lunch break from 1:20 PM to 2:00 PM.
- Each period is 1hr minutes.
- The academic calendar (semester start, internal test dates, exam dates) is published by the college at the start of every semester and displayed on department notice boards.
- Odd semesters usually run from August to December, and even semesters from February to June.

## 6. Library
- Library timings: 8:30 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday to Saturday. Closed on Sundays and public holidays.
- Students can borrow up to 3 books at a time for 14 days.
- Late return fine: Rs. 2 per day per book.
- A valid college ID card is required to borrow books.
- The library has a reading hall, a digital section with e-journals, and a reference section where books cannot be taken out.
- Lost books must be replaced or paid for at the current price.

## 7. Hostel (SAMPLE VALUES)
- Separate hostels are available for boys and girls.
- Hostel fee per year is Rs. 80,000, including mess charges.
- Hostel gate closing time: 9:00 PM for all residents.
- Visitors are allowed only on Sundays between 10:00 AM and 5:00 PM with warden permission.
- Ragging is strictly prohibited and punishable as per law.

## 8. Facilities
- Transport: college buses run on major routes in Bengaluru. Bus pass is available at the transport office.
- Canteen: open from 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM.
- Wi-Fi is available in the library and academic blocks for students.
- Sports: cricket, football, basketball, volleyball, and a gymnasium.
- Placement cell: conducts aptitude training, mock interviews, and campus drives from the pre-final year.

## 9. Rules and conduct
- Wearing the college ID card is mandatory on campus.
- Use of mobile phones in classrooms is not allowed during lectures.
- Any complaint about harassment or ragging should be reported to the anti-ragging committee or the principal's office immediately.
